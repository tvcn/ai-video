ARTICLE Prompt Structure: Subject, Action, Space, CameraBuild prompts in four parts: who, doing what, where, camera. Stable order gives stable output. ARTICLE AI Shot List: One Prompt, One ShotOne prompt, one shot. Three events in one prompt become a random montage. ARTICLE Text to Video: Write Shots, Not AdjectivesWrite shots and actions, not "cinematic, epic"; the model cannot act on adjectives. ARTICLE AI Character Sheet: Lock the DescriptionLock five fields: face, age, hair, wardrobe, temperament. Carry them into every generation. ARTICLE Hybrid Workflow: Lock Live Action FirstLock live action before generating missing shots. Generate first and the light will never match.
