Editorial rules

Zero decorative images

No AI hero, stock photo, mood image, flowchart or icon illustration. If there is no real screenshot, there is no image. Text, tables and copyable prompts carry the information.

No "strongest/best"

Only "best under these conditions". Every model and tool has a failure case. Cards carry a read date; prices and access follow the official page.

Cloud first, local later

No 16GB GPU, no ComfyUI recommendation. No 24GB, no local H3 2K. Validate in the cloud, then cut cost locally.

Auto-edit saves chores, not directing

AI can transcribe, rough-cut, caption and repurpose. Selecting a performance, building narrative and mixing remain human.
