Minimum viable stack

Beginner: one cloud generator + Jianying/CapCut + phone mic. That is enough for a 15s product clip or a talking head. Add ElevenLabs only if the voice is the bottleneck. Add ComfyUI only when you need repeatability, privacy or no per-second cost.

Buying rule

Do not buy an annual plan before you have exported five videos with the free tier. Price per usable second, not per month: a cheap plan with 30% success costs more than an expensive plan with 80% success.

Prerequisites

  • Your route from /en/start/route/
  • A budget ceiling
  • A machine spec

Steps

  1. Pick one generator and one editor.
  2. Export a 5s test from each.
  3. Add one audio tool only if needed.
  4. Write the stack and the monthly cost.
  5. Name one backup per category.
  6. Delete or pause everything else for 7 days.

Checks

  • Stack has three tools, not ten.
  • You know the monthly cost.
  • You know each tool's worst case.
  • You have one backup, not five.
