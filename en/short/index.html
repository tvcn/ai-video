Short-form AI video: 15s, product and talking-head recipes

Three short formats, three different jobs. Pick one, run three shots, and do not turn a 15-second clip into a ten-shot trailer. Each recipe below is a starting structure, not a model demo.

Three recipes, one job each

A 15s short sells one thing. A product film proves one physical detail. A talking head delivers one claim. If you cannot say the job in one sentence, the edit will not fix it.

15s short: hook, demo, payoff

The first three seconds carry the whole clip. If the result is not visible or audible by 3s, rewrite the hook before generating more shots.

TimeShotActionSound
0–3sCloseState the result; no logo, no introVO first
3–8sMediumOne action that proves the claimFoley + VO
8–15sClosePayoff; leave caption safe areaOne closing line

Product film: label, hand, result

Do not ask the model to render label text. Shoot the real label or add typography in the edit.

Talking head: hook, proof, CTA

Front light, short sentences, one camera angle. A side-face avatar with a covered mouth is a lip-sync failure waiting to happen.

TimeShotActionSound
0–5sClose / frontFirst line is the result, not a bioVO first
5–20sMediumOne verifiable action or screen resultVO + room tone
20–30sClose / frontOne CTA; leave caption spaceVO lands

Choose one and run it

Do not produce all three in one day. Choose the recipe that matches the deliverable; run the first test; log face, hands, text, audio sync, time and cost; then decide whether the format is viable.

Prerequisites

  • One platform and one aspect ratio.
  • One subject: a product, a face, or a result.
  • A 5s test budget and a retry cap.
  • Read /en/lab/ if you have not seen the three recipe timelines.

Steps

  1. Write the job in one sentence: sell, prove, or explain.
  2. Pick the matching recipe and copy its three shots.
  3. Set 5s / 720p / 3 takes for the first test.
  4. Log face, hands, text, audio sync, time and cost.
  5. Cut the best take to 15s; do not add a fourth shot.
  6. Export with captions and check the first frame.

Copy-ready prompts / settings

15s short control prompt
Subject: ____ (one product or one person).
Job: ____ (one result the viewer must understand).
Shot: locked-off medium close; subject fills half the frame.
Action: one action completed in 5s.
Light: soft window light; clean background.
Sound: room tone only; no music.
Output: 5s, 9:16, 720p; three takes.
Negative: extra hands, label text, camera shake, background jump.
Product label shot
Macro shot of the real product label, label fills half the frame, one hand enters from frame right, hold 0.5s, soft side light, no generated text, 5s, 9:16.

Checks

  • The clip has one job, not three.
  • The first three seconds show or say the result.
  • There are exactly three shots.
  • The first test was 5s / 720p / 3 takes.
  • Label text was shot or added in post, not generated.
