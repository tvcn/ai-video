Short-form / Task guide

Your first 15-second vertical video

15 seconds is not a shortened ad. It is one hook, one demo, one payoff. Generate 3 shots, keep 1, add a human voice and phone-readable captions.

15s structure

TimeBeatDoDo not
0–3sHookShow the result or the problemLogo intro
3–6sContextOne sentence why it mattersHistory
6–11sDemoOne action, one proofFive features
11–15sPayoffResult + one CTATwo CTAs

Three shots, not ten

Shot 1: hook close-up. Shot 2: demo/product in use. Shot 3: result/reaction. If a shot does not add information, cut it. Three shots at 5s each is a complete 15s video.

Sound-off test

A stranger should understand the video with the sound off. Put the key word on screen in the first 3 seconds. Captions: 18–24 characters per line, safe area, no more than two lines.

Make three versions

Same footage, three hooks: result-first, problem-first, question-first. Publish privately and compare 3-second retention. Do not judge by likes alone.

Prerequisites

  • One product/topic
  • One hook sentence
  • Phone or cloud generator
  • Editor

Steps

  1. Write one hook sentence.
  2. Write three shots with one purpose each.
  3. Generate or shoot 3 clips.
  4. Pick the best take of each.
  5. Add voice and captions.
  6. Cut to 15s; check safe area.
  7. Export 1080×1920.
  8. Make 3 hook variants.

Copy-ready prompts / settings

15s product prompt
Shot 1 (0–3s): macro of the product label, hand enters, natural window light.
Shot 2 (3–8s): product used on a desk, one clear action.
Shot 3 (8–15s): result close-up, clean background, space for captions.
9:16, 1080p, realistic, no invented text, no logo.

Checks

  • Hook is visible in 3s.
  • Only one CTA.
  • Sound-off understandable.
  • Captions within safe area.
  • No logo intro.
  • 15s exactly.
