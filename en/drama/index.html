Make a 90-second AI drama with one person

A 90s drama is 16 shots, 2 characters, 6–10 references, one location, one conflict. Do not start with a 10-minute film. Start with one 15s proof scene.

Output90s 1080×1920 MP4 + SRT + character bible + shot list.
Time2–4 days
Cost¥150–500
LevelIntermediate
Not forYou have one afternoon and no script.

Six-stage pipeline

StageOutputGate
1 ScriptLogline + 8 beatsConflict in one sentence
2 Storyboard16-shot listEvery shot has a purpose
3 CharacterBible + refsSame face across 3 angles
4 Shots16 clips3 usable per shot
5 AudioVoice + music + SFXLip and loudness
6 Edit90s cut + QCPhone test

Cost formula

Final seconds × price per second × retry factor + voice + music/SFX + human edit time. Example: 90s × ¥0.50 × 4 = ¥180, plus ¥30 voice and ¥20 music, before your time. If your retry factor is 8, fix the shots before adding more.

First gate: the 15s proof

Before generating 16 shots, make one 15s scene with both characters, one dialogue line and one reaction. If the faces do not match or the audio is unusable, stop and fix the character bible. Do not 'fix it in the edit'.

Prerequisites

  • One logline
  • Two characters
  • One location
  • A cloud video account
  • An editor

Steps

  1. Write a one-sentence logline.
  2. Break into 8 beats / 16 shots.
  3. Build a character bible with 6–10 refs.
  4. Generate a 15s proof scene.
  5. Fix faces/audio before scaling.
  6. Generate remaining shots in batches.
  7. Assemble, voice, music, subtitles.
  8. QC and export.

Copy-ready prompts / settings

15s proof scene
Two characters, a small rented room at night, one table, one window.
C01 says: “You knew it all along, didn't you?”
C02 looks away, then back: “I was going to tell you.”
Shot 1: C01 close-up. Shot 2: C02 medium. Shot 3: both in wide, silence.
5s each, 9:16, realistic, low saturation, no music.

Checks

  • Logline has a conflict.
  • 16 shots each have a purpose.
  • Faces match across 3 angles.
  • Dialogue is audible on phone.
  • No shot is a decorative filler.
  • Cost per final second is known.
