Pick your first AI video route in 10 minutes

Four questions decide your route: what you want to make, who appears, how long, and whether you will pay. Do not start with a model; start with a deliverable.

Four questions, in order

QuestionIf yesIf no
Do you need a person on camera?Talking head / drama / character routeProduct / landscape / mood route
Do you need a story with multiple shots?Drama route: script > storyboard > shotsShort route: one hook, one shot
Do you need exact product/label text?Shoot or use H3/Seedance plus post typographyPure AI generation
Can you spend ¥100–300 this week?Cloud-first, 1080p, more retriesLocal 5B / free tiers, lower resolution

Three routes

RouteBest forFirst outputAvoid
A. Drama (90s)Story, 2 characters, 16 shots15s proof scene, then 90sStarting with a 10-minute film
B. Short-formProduct, talking head, 15s hookOne 15s vertical videoCopying viral edits without a hook
C. Brand / moodMV, travel, atmosphereOne 5s hero shot + one 15s cutNo narrative, only pretty frames

Seven-day plan

DayDoDone when
1Write one sentence + one referenceYou can say what 'done' looks like
2Install one editor + one generatorYou can export a 5s clip
3Generate 5 shots, keep 1You know your failure rate
4Add voice / music / subtitlesYou can watch it on a phone
5Reshoot or regenerate the weakest shotOne shot improves, not all
6Export and QC12-point check passes
7Publish privately, write 5 linesYou know cost and next fix

Kill criteria

If three attempts on the same shot fail for the same reason, change the shot design, not the model. If the whole route misses the deadline twice, cut scope: 90s to 30s, 16 shots to 6. Do not extend the deadline silently.

Prerequisites

  • One concrete deliverable, not 'learn AI video'.
  • A deadline within 7 days.
  • A budget ceiling you can afford to lose.
  • A machine you actually have (phone / 8GB laptop / 16GB GPU / cloud).

Steps

  1. Answer the four questions in one line each.
  2. Pick route A, B or C; write why.
  3. Write the deliverable, length, aspect ratio and deadline.
  4. List the exact tools you will use, no more than three.
  5. Set a budget ceiling and a retry cap.
  6. Write three kill criteria.
  7. Put the route card where you will see it.

Checks

  • You can name one route, not three.
  • Deliverable has a length and a platform.
  • Tool list has no more than three tools.
  • Budget and retry cap are written down.
  • Kill criteria are specific.
