About / AI Video Field Guide

About and editorial standards

This site answers one question: when AI video tools, models and platforms keep changing, how do you finish a film that can actually be delivered?

What this site is

The AI Video Field Guide is TVCN's ninth desk. It does not chase the “strongest model”. It puts task, tool, settings, cost, failure and checks on one page: state the deliverable first, then decide cloud or local, which model, and what budget. Pages are organised by job, not by vendor.

How we write

  • Deliverable, time box and budget come before the tool.
  • Tool and model cards state price, limits, licence and last-checked date. Prices and quotas change; check the official page before you run it.
  • Every task page has steps, copy-ready prompts/settings, a check list and a failure table. Without these, it is not a complete task page.
  • Official screenshots are labelled “official public page”; real footage is labelled “not AI-generated”; unlabelled images are illustrative.

Where AI stops

We do not generate fake client cases or fake measurements. Model output is illustrative, not an on-set test. For likeness, copyright, platform rules and commercial licensing, the official terms and a qualified lawyer govern. “Free / open source / see official” is a finding label, not a licensing conclusion.

Versions and corrections

Each page carries an edited date. When tool prices, model versions, platform policies or regional availability change, we update that page first, not on a calendar. If you find an error, send the URL, the exact sentence and the evidence to the editorial inbox. We mark corrections on the page instead of silently rewriting it.
